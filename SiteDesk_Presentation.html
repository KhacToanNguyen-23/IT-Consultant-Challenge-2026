03 · Solve: Operational Workflow
SiteDesk: AI Accelerates Lookup, Humans Retain the Final Decision
A 4-step human-in-the-loop operational pipeline converting chaos into clean orders
1. READ (AI)
Uploads PDF/Excel or pastes text.
Extracts items, quantities, and units in DA, NO, SV, EN.
2. MATCH (AI)
Semantic search maps messy trade text to SKUs with confidence scores (>80%).
3. SUBSTITUTE (AI)
Real-time store stock check.
Suggests up to 2 equal/better spec in-stock alternatives.
4. CONFIRM (HUMAN)
Staff reviews in <30s, confirms with customer, and pushes clean order to POS/ERP.
Measurable Operational Shift
• Lookup Time: ~12 min manual search → <2 min total.
• Stockout Answer: "Sorry, out of stock" → "Approved equivalent right here".
• Service Quality: Depends on who is on shift → 100% consistent advice.
Beyond Software: People & Process
• Nordic Counter Standard: Staff measured on accuracy & saved sales, not raw volume.
• Product Data Stewardship: Dedicated data owners maintaining catalog specs.
• No Headcount Cuts: Time saved is returned to active customer consulting.
