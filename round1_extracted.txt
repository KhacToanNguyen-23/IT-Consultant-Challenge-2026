Business Challenges
Over time, each country and region has developed its own 
systems and manual processes to meet local needs.
This has created several challenges:
◼ Manual processes
Create inefficiencies and require significant employee effort.
◼ Inconsistent operations
Different ways of working can lead to inconsistent store 
operations and customer experiences.
◼ Delivery & fulfilment
Challenges with orders and deliveries can affect efficiency 
and reliability.
◼ Fragmented systems
Different systems across countries make it difficult to scale 
improvements across the business.
The challenges are interconnected, but their impact and 
urgency differ from one market to another.

Different market, different 
priorities
The most critical challenge is not necessarily the same 
in every country.
For example:
◼ Denmark
Manual processes may create the greatest impact.
◼ Norway
Store operations may be a more urgent priority.
◼ Sweden
Delivery and order fulfilment may have greater 
business impact.
At the same time, Company A operates in a highly 
competitive market.
Failing to address the most critical challenges could 
affect the company's ability to compete and serve its 
customers effectively.
The question is: Where should Company A focus first?

One solution. Different 
realities
The executive board wants to invest in one digital 
solution that can deliver the greatest business impact.
However, the solution cannot simply be rolled out in 
exactly the same way across all three countries.
Your solution needs to consider:
◼ Local processes and business requirements
◼ Existing systems and technology
◼ Different operational needs
◼ Store and distribution operations
◼ The impact of change on employees and customers
◼ Different implementation and rollout approaches
◼ One strategic solution does not necessarily mean one 
identical implementation.

Your mission as IT 
consultants
The company cannot solve every challenge at once.
Your team, as IT consultants, needs to make the right strategic 
choices and focus on one opportunity that can realistically deliver 
meaningful impact to help Company A.
◼ 01:  Identify
Choose ONE key business opportunity to address.
◼ 02: Prioritise
Explain why this opportunity should be prioritised over other 
challenges.
◼ 03: Solve
Propose a digital solution and a focused MVP scope that 
demonstrates its potential business impact.
◼ 04: Scale
Consider how the solution could be rolled out across different 
markets, taking local differences and implementation constraints 
into account.
 Time to market is critical.
◼ Scope carefully. Focus on one problem. Maximise the impact.

Tips for you

--- PAGE 16 ---
Tips
◼ You may make reasonable assumptions where information is not provided. Clearly state and justify your 
assumptions.
◼ This case is intentionally open-ended. Success is not about finding the "right" problem but about 
selecting a high-impact opportunity and demonstrating a logical, well-structured digital solution to 
address it.
◼ Keep time to market in mind when defining the scope of your MVP
◼ Food for thought: 
◼ Which business problem did you prioritise?
◼ Who experiences it? 
◼ Why is it important? 
◼ What solution do you propose? 
◼ What is the scope of your MVP?
◼ Where and how could AI create value in your proposed solution?
