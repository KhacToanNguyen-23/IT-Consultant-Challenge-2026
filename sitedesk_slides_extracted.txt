FIVE WHYS
1 Why do customers wait ~12 min and leave with gaps?  → Every line is  searched and re-keyed by hand.
2 Why by hand?  → Legacy POS/ERP systems, different in each country, were  built for checkout, not for reading a customer’s list.
3 Why does the result depend on who serves you?  → Product-equivalence  know-how lives in experienced people’s heads, not in a system.
4 Why hasn’t it been fixed?  → Each market built its own tools; the cost is  spread over hundreds of stores and invisible at group level.
5 Root cause: no shared, data-driven way to turn a customer’s list into  an order  – so every counter improvises.
SO WHAT Software alone won’t fix it – the counter needs one standard, shared product data  and a tool that makes them  the default.
